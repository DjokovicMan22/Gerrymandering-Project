# Week 7 final-deliverable standard

The final paper should not merely list outputs. It should make a defensible argument.

## Required argument structure

1. Research question.
2. Hypothesis.
3. Why an ensemble baseline is necessary.
4. Data and graph model.
5. Metrics and their limitations.
6. ReCom method.
7. Diagnostics.
8. Main outlier result.
9. Sorted-district structural evidence.
10. Representative maps.
11. Robustness checks.
12. Limitations and strongest counterargument.
13. Conclusion with bounded claims.

## Minimum evidence standard

The paper should cite these project-generated tables and figures:

- Week 4 multi-chain diagnostics.
- Week 5 outlier percentile table.
- Week 5 signature outlier panel.
- Week 5 sorted-district plot.
- Week 5 representative map comparison.
- Week 6 constraint sensitivity table.
- Week 6 constraint percentile figure.
- Week 6 geography baseline table and figure.

## Claim discipline

Use this wording pattern:

> Under the stated data, graph model, ReCom proposal, population constraint, burn-in choice, and scoring election, the enacted plan falls at the reported empirical percentile of the sampled ensemble.

Avoid these claims unless you add separate evidence:

- The map is illegal.
- The map proves intent.
- The chain perfectly mixed.
- The ensemble represents every legally valid plan.
- A single metric proves gerrymandering.
